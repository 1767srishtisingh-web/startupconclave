Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about the conclave? Write to us at idealab@akgec.ac.in.

Who can register for Startup Conclave '26?

Participation is open to eligible students, early-stage startups, independent innovators, and entrepreneurs.

What are the key benefits and prize pool?

Participants get access to seed funding, pre-incubation support at AKGEC IDEA Lab, IPR legal guidance, and live pitching to investors.

Do I need an already-established company to attend?

No. You can participate with just an early concept or prototype, or register as a delegate to attend keynotes, interact with VCs, and network with fellow innovators.

How does the Pitching Competition work?

Shortlisted teams pitch live to our investor and jury panel on Day 2. The jury evaluates according to fixed criteria and selects top teams for prizes and incubation support.

How does the Startup Exhibition work?

20 startup stalls showcase working prototypes and products on Day 1 (2:15 PM – 5:30 PM) with mentor walkthroughs and attendee interaction.

What is the last date to register?

Registration officially closes on 10 October 2026 at 11:59 PM IST, and the results for Round 1 will be announced on 12 October 2026. Register early as slots are limited.
