Why Attend

Why just have an idea? When you can build it. Pitch it. Grow it.

Startup Conclave 2026 brings together ideas, innovators, mentors, investors and opportunities — all under one roof.

Big ventures often begin with one small idea — and the courage to put it out there.

  1. ~₹50,000 Prize Pool

    Your idea could be worth more than you think. Pitch, compete, and get recognized in front of seasoned industry evaluators.

  2. Pre-Incubation Opportunity

    A great idea needs the right environment to grow. Selected teams get the opportunity to move towards pre-incubation and further development.

  3. IPR Guidance

    Your idea. Your innovation. Your intellectual property. Get direct exposure to IPR guidance and understand how innovative ideas can be protected.

  4. Seed-Funding Opportunities

    Have an idea with potential? Explore opportunities to take it from a concept towards reality, subject to selection and applicable criteria.

  5. Take The Stage

    Stop explaining your idea only to the people around you. Put it in front of a jury. Pitch your startup, showcase your vision and receive expert feedback.

  6. Meet The Ecosystem

    Connect with founders, investors, mentors, industry experts and fellow innovators. Because sometimes, one conversation can change the direction of an idea.

  3. 3. Team Size: Follow the team size rules specified by the organizers.
  4. 4. Slot Allocation: Registering does not guarantee a slot for exhibition or pitching; teams will be selected based on eligibility and evaluation.
  5. 5. Punctuality: Reach the event venue on time.
  6. 6. Adherence: Follow the event schedule and instructions given by the team.
  7. 7. IP Ownership: Your idea remains yours. You are responsible for protecting your intellectual property (IP) before sharing it publicly.
  8. 8. Schedule Flexibility: Organizers can change the schedule, process, or venue setup if needed.
  9. 9. Jury Decisions: Decisions made by the judges during the pitching competition are final and based on fixed criteria.
  10. 10. Support Criteria: Mentorship, incubation support, IP assistance, and funding options depend on selection and rules; they are not guaranteed just by joining.
  11. 11. Code of Conduct: Maintain proper discipline and behavior throughout the event.
  12. 12. Agreement: By registering, you agree to follow all rules and guidelines set by the team.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about the conclave? Write to us at idealab@akgec.ac.in.

Who can register for Startup Conclave '26?

Participation is open to eligible students, early-stage startups, independent innovators, and entrepreneurs. Students register with their college email; everyone else can use their primary email.

What are the key benefits and prize pool?

Participants get access to the ₹50,000 prize pool, pre-incubation opportunities at AKGEC IDEA Lab, IPR legal guidance, seed-funding opportunities, and live pitching to investors.

Do I need an already-established company to attend?

No. You can participate with just an early concept or prototype, or attend as a delegate to learn from keynotes, interact with VCs, and network with fellow innovators.

How does the Pitching Competition work?

Shortlisted teams pitch live to our investor and jury panel on Day 2. The jury evaluates according to fixed criteria and selects top teams for prizes and incubation support.

How does the Startup Exhibition work?

30+ startup stalls showcase working prototypes and products on Day 1 (2:15 PM – 5:30 PM) with mentor walkthroughs and attendee interaction.

What is the last date to register?

Registration officially closes on 10 October 2026 at 11:59 PM IST. Register early as slots are limited.
