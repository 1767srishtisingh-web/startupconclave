From a rough idea to a funded startup

Startup Conclave is AKGEC’s annual gathering for people building something new. Organised by the AKGEC IDEA Lab, the college’s technical business incubator, it puts students, early-stage founders and investors in the same room for two days.

Great ideas don’t wait for the right time. Neither should you.

  1. 1

    Ideate

    Bring an idea, however early. Sharpen the problem it solves in sessions with mentors.

  2. 2

    Innovate

    Make it testable in hands-on workshops on product, prototyping and market fit.

  3. 3

    Ignite

    Pitch live to a jury of investors and founders, and get direct feedback on the spot.

  4. 4

    Inspire

    Hear from founders and operators about what worked, what didn’t, and why.

  5. 5

    Impact

    The most promising startups share up to ₹3 lakh in seed funding to take the next step.

    Questions

    Can’t find what you need? Write to idealab@akgec.ac.in.

    Who can register?

    Students from any college, early-stage startups, independent innovators and entrepreneurs. Students register with their college email; everyone else can use any email.

    Do I need a startup to attend?

    No. Most attendees come to learn and meet people. You only need an idea, and not even that, if you just want to listen and network.

    How does the pitch competition work?

    Round 1 is on Day 1: five minutes to pitch and three minutes of questions. The jury shortlists teams for the finals on Day 2, and the most promising startups share up to ₹3 lakh in seed funding.

    We are a team. How do we register?

    Each member registers separately with the same startup or idea name, so we can group you. Only one member needs to tick “I want to pitch”.

    What should I bring?

    A valid photo ID. If you are pitching, bring a laptop and your deck on a USB drive plus a cloud link as backup.

    What is the last date to register?

    10 October 2026. Seats are limited, so registering early is a good idea.
